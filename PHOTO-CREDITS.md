## Why stock at all

Fioredano's own photography on Facebook, Google, Yelp and Houzz could not be
retrieved: Facebook photo pages require a logged-in session, and Yelp, Houzz and
Google all refused automated access. `fioredanoconstruction.com` is a parked
Namecheap domain with no site behind it. Replacing these with real job photos is
the single highest-value change you can make to this site.
