How it works

Four steps, one price, no surprises.

  1. Tell us about the room

    Call, or send the form below. We'll ask what's there now, what you want instead, and roughly when you'd like it done.

  2. Free in-home estimate

    Andrew comes out, measures, and looks at what's behind the walls. You get a written, itemized price — and if you're holding a competing written quote, we'll beat or match it.

  3. We build it

    Floors and furniture get covered before anything comes apart. Same crew start to finish, and the site gets swept at the end of every day.

  4. Walkthrough and punch list

    We walk the finished room together. Anything you flag gets fixed before we call the job done and collect the balance.

About the timeline: a standard hall bathroom is usually 2–3 weeks from demo to walkthrough. A full master suite runs longer. We'll give you real dates in writing, not a guess on the phone.
